About StudyFlow

StudyFlow is a study planning web application designed to help students manage courses, tasks, deadlines, and study progress in one place.

Instead of simply storing a list of tasks, StudyFlow uses deadlines, task importance, progress, and workload to help students understand what needs their attention first.
